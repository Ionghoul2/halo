    Installation

    How it goes on. And comes off.

    1. Prep the surface

      Wash and dry the panels. Wipe each mounting point with the included prep wipes.

    2. Mount the strands

      Clips and tape matched to your panel material, over protective film where needed. Magnets won't hold on aluminum, fiberglass or plastic.

    3. Route and power

      Run leads along seams to a fused power system sized for the real load.

    4. Set up the controller

      Pair the remote or app, pick colors or effects. Mapping kits add a layout step.

    5. Remove and store

      Peel slowly, clean residue, coil strands on the included reels.

    Reference footage. Shows other installers' methods, not ours.

    Power and safety

    Thousands of LEDs draw real current. Every kit's power system will be designed and fused for its actual load and reviewed before sale. Never run hundreds of watts through a cigarette-lighter socket. We'll publish install times once we've timed real installs.

    Before you glow.

    Can I drive with the lights on?

    Car Glow kits are made for parked displays, photos and private-property events. Many places restrict colored or flashing exterior lights on moving vehicles. Check your local laws before driving lit.

    Will it damage my paint?

    We're choosing mounting materials panel by panel and testing removal. We won't promise zero damage on every finish. Clean surfaces, the included film and slow removal matter most.

    How do you pick my size?

    Mostly by vehicle length: Small up to 15 ft, Medium up to 17 ft, Large up to 20 ft. Over 20 ft, vans and unusual shapes get a manual fitment check.

    Can I upload my own graphics?

    That's the goal for Graphic Mapping. It stays a concept until we've proven the full workflow on the hardware we ship.

    Does underglow sync with the body lights?

    Not by default. Underglow runs on its own controller unless we test and confirm sync.

    When can I buy?

    Join early access. We open orders kit by kit once each one's parts, power system and shipping are confirmed.
