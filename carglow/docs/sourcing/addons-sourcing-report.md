## Vehicle compatibility (all estimates)

- **Underglow:** Small fits 13–15 ft vehicles, Medium 15–17 ft, Large 17–20 ft. Measure the rocker panel between the wheel wells; the side strip has to fit. Use when parked or on private property; Georgia restricts road use.
- **Eyes:** needs about 120 cm (47 in) of clear windshield width. Parked display only.
- **Interior:** universal 12V, designed for 4-door cars. Door strips are 75 cm.

None of the three suppliers lists vehicle fitment beyond "universal", so every fit stays labelled an estimate.
